### What changed since v2.0.0
- **Hints** (~1,100 notes): complete sentences from the reading passages, no stray spaces before punctuation, balanced quotation marks, dialogue kept together with its speaker, story titles no longer glued to the sentence, very short hints get the previous sentence for context.
- **Persian translations** (~1,070 notes): a full review of all Meaning/Example translations — truncated and broken translations completed, untranslated English words and stray non-Persian characters removed, Persian ی/ک instead of Arabic ي/ك, consistent final punctuation. Translations stay faithful to the book's sentences.
- **English typos**: e.g. "infame" → "inflame", a stray leading period in the "drain" example; the hint of "found" now uses the word in its own sense ("I founded this bank").
- **Media**: every image and audio file was verified against its checksum.
