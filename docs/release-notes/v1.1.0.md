### Already studying these decks? Your progress is safe
This update keeps every note ID (GUID), the note type and its fields exactly as in v1.0.0 and on AnkiWeb (verified note by note against the AnkiWeb downloads). Importing it only updates the text of existing cards; your review history, intervals and due dates stay as they are.

How to update:
1. Download the `4000EEW_Old_Book_N.apkg` files you use and open them with Anki (File → Import).
2. Keep the default import options (**Update notes: If newer**). Do not delete your old decks first.

### What changed since v1.0.0
- **Hints** (~1,080 notes): complete sentences from the reading passages, no stray spaces before punctuation, balanced quotation marks, dialogue kept together with its speaker, very short hints get the previous sentence for context, and the 10 notes that showed "Context not found." now have a hint.
- **Typos** in ~30 definitions, examples and hints, e.g. "ora job" → "or a job", "o f" → "of", "Asa" → "As a", "s tiff" → "stiff", "litthe" → "lit the", "ofthe" → "of the", "a period often years" → "a period of ten years", "a a state" → "a state"; the empty definition of "web" was filled in.
- **Media** (Book 4): corrected audio for "texture", "shrink" and "subject" and a corrected image for "snap".
- Book 4: the hand-edited hint of "beware" from the AnkiWeb version is kept as it is.
