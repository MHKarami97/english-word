### Already studying these decks? Your progress is safe
Note IDs (GUIDs), the note type and its fields are unchanged (checked against the AnkiWeb versions), so importing only switches your cards to the new media files — your review history, intervals and due dates stay as they are. Keep Anki's default import options (**Update notes: If newer**) and do not delete your old decks first.

The previous image and audio files stay in your collection after the import. To free that space, use **Tools → Check Media → Delete Unused** in Anki (optional).
